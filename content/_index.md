This website presents the Department of Computer Science's plans for a restructured foundational Computer Science core. This is a two-year course progression incorporating a spiral curriculum design approach that attempts to modernize and streamline core computer science learning, emphasize the development of systems thinking (including humanistic aspects and human interactions with computational systems). This two-year core will serve as a shared starting point for CS specialization degrees (Cybersecurity, AI) and is designed with community college transfer and service courses to other departments in mind.
