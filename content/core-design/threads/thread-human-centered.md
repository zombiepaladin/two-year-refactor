> *Working draft for faculty review. A **spiral** deepens through escalating passes across the two-year core. This is the spiral; one of eight spirals (with three lenses and one bounded practice — twelve threads total, plus two cross-cutting norms).*

**In one line:** Computing in service of human needs: design FOR, communicate TO, and validate WITH people — perceive → represent → comprehend → defend → fitness-for-purpose.

---

## Character

Carries two embedded throughlines (accessibility + data visualization) and owns validation-with-users (acceptance testing, A/B testing), which is the "is it what they needed" question — distinct from the "is it correct" question that Correctness & Verification keeps.

## Passes across the two years

| Course | What's new at this pass |
|---|---|
| **CIS 120** (Y1 Fall) — Web Foundations | Accessibility basics + visual hierarchy as human comprehension (PERCEIVE). |
| **CIS 320** (Y2 Fall) — User Experience Development | Honest data visualization — represent data for humans (REPRESENT), on the client side of the CIS 300/320 pairing. |
| **CIS 260** (Y1 Spring) — Foundations of Relational Databases | Visualization as investigation — comprehend data (COMPREHEND). |
| **CIS 400** (Y2 Spring) — capstone of the core | Defend human-facing choices; introduce acceptance criteria (what makes this acceptable to the user?). |
| **CIS 320** (Y2 Fall), continued | Formal, bounded exposure to acceptance-criteria and A/B-testing process work (FITNESS) — every student gets this once. `cs.md` is explicit that a *full* acceptance-testing deep dive is deliberately deferred to a Software Architecture specialization elective instead, not duplicated here. |

## Connections

- Data-viz throughline converges with the Trustworthy Computing lens (honest charts). CIS 400's defend pass sits beside Correctness & Verification — two kinds of "does it work" (fitness vs correctness) converge but answer different questions.

## Open questions for faculty review

1. Data visualization is embedded, never a course slot — confirm faculty can see/assess it.
2. Confirm the two throughlines (accessibility + data viz) read clearly as one thread.
3. CIS 320 now carries both the data-visualization (REPRESENT) and acceptance-testing (FITNESS) passes on a 1-credit course — worth watching alongside the program's other density flags (CIS 115, CIS 260, CIS 300), though smaller in scale than those.
