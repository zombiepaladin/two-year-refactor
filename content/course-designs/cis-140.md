## Course Description

Hands-on introduction to Artificial Intelligence (AI). This course explores foundational topics like data handling, supervised and unsupervised learning, neural networks, natural language processing, computer vision, and the ethical implications of AI. Interactive labs and projects are used to develop real AI applications and reflect on their impact in society.

This course.  

## Course Goals and Learning Outcomes

1. Describe the fundamental concepts of artificial intelligence and data science and their roles in modern society.
2. Identify common applications of AI and data science across a variety of disciplines and industries.
3. Explain the basic data science workflow, including data collection, preparation, analysis, and interpretation.
4. Use introductory data analysis tools to explore and visualize datasets.
5. Interpret and communicate insights derived from data analyses and AI-generated outputs.
6. Recognize ethical, legal, and societal considerations associated with AI and data-driven decision making.
7. Apply generative AI tools responsibly to support problem-solving, information analysis, and content creation.
8. Evaluate the strengths and limitations of AI systems and data-driven models.
