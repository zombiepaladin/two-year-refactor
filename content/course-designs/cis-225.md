## Course Description

A new course created for the two-year core redesign — no existing K-State catalog entry. Coverage of the OSI seven-layer model, with exploration of HTTP/HTTPS and web sockets, and some packet structuring. Physical and Data Link layers are conceptual-only; full depth is deferred to Computer Engineering.

## Credit Hours

1 credit (per `resources/reference/degree-maps/cs.md`)
