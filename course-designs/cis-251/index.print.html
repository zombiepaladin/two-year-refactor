CIS 251 - Foundations of Cybersecurity

Course Description

This course introduces the fundamental concepts of cybersecurity, including how digital systems communicate, common cyber threats, security principles, risk management, ethical considerations, and career opportunities in the field. Students will develop a cybersecurity mindset through case studies, discussions, and hands-on activities.

Credit Hours

1 credit

Course Overview

This course serves as a basic introduction to cybersecurity with a focus on exploring common threats and mitigations. Students will learn key principles such as the CIA triad, authentication, authorization, encryption, trust, and more. The course will include case studies of common cybersecurity threats and incidents and discussions on how to prevent them in the future. Students should leave the course with a defensive mindset and an understanding of risks and vulnerabilities present in modern technology.

Course Goals and Learning Outcomes

Upon successful completion of the course, students will be able to:

  1. Explain the importance of cybersecurity in modern society.
  2. Describe key cybersecurity principles, including the CIA Triad.
  3. Identify common cyber threats and attack methods.
  4. Analyze cybersecurity risks and potential impacts.
  5. Describe defensive security measures and best practices.
  6. Discuss legal and ethical issues in cybersecurity.
  7. Evaluate cybersecurity incidents and recommend mitigations.
