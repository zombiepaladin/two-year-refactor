# B.S. in Digital Innovation in Media (DIGIN) — Course List

**Source:** [K-State A.Q. Miller School of Media and Communication](https://www.k-state.edu/media-communication/academics/bs-digital-innovation-media.html)

120 total credit hours. Four-year program earning: a Bachelor's degree in Digital Innovation in Media, a Minor in Entrepreneurship and Innovation, a Certificate in Computer Science, and a Certificate in Digital Engagement.
