## Program Requirements (18 credit hours)

- **Outside Concentration (9 hours):** an outside credential of at least 9 hours (any unit except Statistics and Data Science), OR 9 hours in a single course prefix other than STAT (300-level+ or with an in-prefix prerequisite; up to 5 hours may overlap with the program), OR 9 hours in a microcredential (up to 5 hours may overlap).
- **Overlays (9 hours, may double-count with K-State Core, Outside Concentration, electives, or the major):**
  - Ethical Reasoning Overlay (3 hours)
  - Global Perspectives Overlay (3 hours)
  - US Multicultural Overlay (3 hours)

## Statistics Major Requirements (51–54 credit hours)

### Introductory Seminar (1 credit hour)
- STAT 150 — Statistics and Data Science: Introductory Seminar for Majors

### Introductory Statistics I (3 credit hours) — choose one
- STAT 225 — Introduction to Statistics
- STAT 240 — Biometrics I
- STAT 250 — Business and Economic Statistics I

### Introductory Statistics II (3 credit hours) — choose one
- STAT 341 — Biometrics II
- STAT 351 — Business and Economic Statistics II

### Introduction to Mathematical Statistics (6 credit hours) — both required
- STAT 610 — Introduction to Mathematical Statistics I
- STAT 611 — Introduction to Mathematical Statistics II

### Design Methods (3 credit hours) — choose one
- STAT 720 — Design of Experiments
- STAT 722 — Experimental Designs for Product Development and Quality Improvement
- *(the current catalog lists a third approved option in this category — confirm with an advisor)*

### Linear Models (3 credit hours)
- STAT 705 — Regression and Analysis of Variance
- *(the current catalog allows a choice between two courses here — confirm the second option with an advisor)*

### Mathematics Foundations (12–13 credit hours)
**Calculus — choose one sequence** (combinations of the two are also acceptable; Math Scholars recommended for dual MATH/STATDS majors):

*Traditional Mathematics (12 hours):*
- MATH 220 — Analytic Geometry and Calculus I
- MATH 221 — Analytic Geometry and Calculus II
- MATH 222 — Analytic Geometry and Calculus III

*OR Scholar Mathematics (13 hours):*
- MATH 221S / equivalent Math Scholars sequence (3 courses)

### Linear Algebra (3–5 credit hours) — choose one
- MATH 551 — Applied Matrix Theory
- MATH 515 — Introduction to Linear Algebra
- (Math Scholars linear algebra equivalent)

### Programming Foundations (3–4 credit hours) — choose one
- CIS 200 — Programming Fundamentals
- CIS 209 — Computer Programming for Engineers

### Advanced Writing Requirement (3 credit hours)
- ENGL 516 — Written Communication for the Sciences

### Statistics Computing (2 credit hours) — both required
- STAT 725 — Introduction to SAS Computing (1 credit)
- STAT 726 — Introduction to R Computing (1 credit)

### Upper-Division Electives (9 credit hours)
**Choose two (6 credit hours) from:**
- ECON 630 — Introduction to Econometrics
- IMSE 541 — Statistical Quality Control
- IMSE 785 — Big Data Analytics
- MIS 665 — Business Analytics and Data Mining
- MIS 670 — Social Media Analytics and Web Mining
- Any MATH 400+ course
- Any STAT 400+ course (except STAT 510, STAT 511, STAT 701, STAT 703)
- Any CIS 400+ course
- Any CC 400+ course

**Plus one additional (3 credit hours):**
- Any STAT course at 700+ level (except STAT 701 and STAT 703)

### Notes
- This is the **B.S.** track; the department also offers a **B.A.** with additional foreign language, humanities, and second-language requirements in place of the BS natural/quantitative and arts/humanities requirements.
- A few elective categories (Design Methods, Linear Models) show slightly different option counts between the official catalog degree map and the program summary page — check with a Statistics department advisor to confirm the full current list of substitutable courses.
