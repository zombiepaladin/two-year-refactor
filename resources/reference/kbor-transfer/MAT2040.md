# MAT 2040 - Discrete Structures

## Transfer Equivalency
This course transfers as MATH 510 - Discrete Math.

## Student Learning Outcomes
Upon completion of this course, students will be able to:
1. Sets, Relations, Functions
    1. Explain basic terminology and operations.
    2. Relate examples to the appropriate set, function, or relation model.  
    3. Calculate the size of a finite set and distinguish finite or infinite sets based on cardinality.
2. Logic and Proof Techniques
    1. Convert statements to propositional and predicate logic expressions.
    2. Apply proof techniques of direct proof, proof by contradiction, weak and strong induction.
    3. Distinguish inductive and recursive definitions and formulas.
3. Permutations, combinations and counting
    1. Apply counting arguments such as the multiplication principle, inclusion-exclusion principle, and pigeonhole principle.
    2. Compute permutations and combinations of a set.
    3. Demonstrate knowledge of binomial coefficients and the Binomial Theorem. 
4. Computational Techniques
    1. Apply recursive definitions and solve basic recurrence relations.
    2. Perform computations involving modular arithmetic.
    3. Use Euclid’s Algorithm and compute the greatest common divisor of two integers.
5. Graph Theory
    1. Describe the basic terminology and properties of graphs with examples.
    2. Work with isomorphic graphs, planar graphs, binary trees, spanning trees.
    3. Convert weighted graphs and their matrix representations.
    4. Apply theorems and algorithms on paths in graphs, including shortest-path problems, Euler paths, Hamiltonian paths.
