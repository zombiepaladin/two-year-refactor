# CIS 115 — Introduction to Computing Science

**Credits:** 2
**Typically offered:** Fall, Spring

A survey of the discipline of Computing Science and its interaction with other disciplines, incorporating historical development, theories, and tools of Computing Science (algorithm design and programming). Topics include: mechanical and digital computers, computational thinking, cybersecurity, simulation, data science, web technologies, and more. Students will be able to explore real-world problems within computer science and research current topics.

**Notes:** 2 hours lecture per week.
