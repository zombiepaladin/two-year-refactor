# CIS 200 — Programming Fundamentals

**Credits:** 4
**Typically offered:** Fall, Spring

Principles of algorithm design and their application to procedural programming: state, control structures, methods. Patterns of conditional and iterative control structure.  Program testing. Introduction to arrays, classes, and objects.  Programming projects.

Prerequisites: CIS 116 (Intro to Programming) and either MATH 205 (General Calc) or Math 220 (Calc I) (either can also
be taken concurrently with CIS 200) with a minimum grade of ‘C’ in each. These prerequisites are considered essential to
student success in the course.
