# CIS 738 — Responsible Use of Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning

**Credits:** 3
**Typically offered:** Spring

The course covers the theory and practice of AI and machine learning ethics. Topics include AI bias, robustness of AI systems, fairness in AI, AI and privacy, safe uses of AI, statistical inference of AI/ML results, and more. The course also includes a final project focused on the analysis of a real-word AI system.
