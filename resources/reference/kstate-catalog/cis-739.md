# CIS 739 — Natural Language Processing

**Credits:** 3
**Typically offered:** Spring

This graduate elective introduces deep learning methods for natural language processing, covering neural architectures such as CNNs, RNNs, transformers, and large language models. Students gain hands-on experience implementing and evaluating NLP models in PyTorch, with emphasis on representation learning, attention, transfer learning, prompting, and real-world applications.
